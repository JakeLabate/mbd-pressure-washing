Method

Low pressure where it matters.

Pressure washing uses high pressure water to lift dirt, grime and buildup from durable surfaces like concrete and brick. Soft washing uses low pressure with specialized cleaning solutions to clean delicate surfaces safely.

Roofs, siding and painted areas are soft washed. That is not a shortcut, it is the only way to clean those surfaces without wearing down the shingle, the paint or the trim underneath.

We use eco friendly methods and adjust the approach to the surface being cleaned.

Soft wash, low pressure

  • Asphalt shingle roofs
  • Vinyl, stucco, brick and Hardie board siding
  • Trim, soffits and painted surfaces

Pressure washing

  • Concrete and brick
  • Paver patios and walkways
  • Driveways and sidewalks

What to expect

Three steps, no surprises.

  1. You get a free estimate

    Tell us about the property and what needs cleaning. The quote is based on your specific needs, and it does not cost anything to ask.

  2. You get told what to move

    Where you can, move vehicles, outdoor furniture and potted plants clear of the areas being cleaned. We may ask for windows to be closed and pets kept indoors. If anything else is needed, you hear it before the day, not on it.

  3. We clean the property

    You do not always need to be home, as long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and an outdoor water source if one is needed. Smaller jobs take a couple of hours. Larger homes and more detailed work take longer, and you get an estimated timeframe with your quote.

Common questions

Answers before you call.

Is pressure washing safe for my home?

Yes, when it is done correctly. We match the equipment, cleaning method and pressure level to each surface, and delicate surfaces are soft washed rather than blasted.

How often should I have my property cleaned?

Most homes benefit from a wash once a year. Some surfaces need it more often depending on weather, shade, tree coverage and buildup.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, on all of our services. Tell us about your property and you get a quote based on what it actually needs.
