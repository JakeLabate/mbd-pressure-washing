How we do it

Deck cleaning done properly.

Decking is where pressure gets people into trouble. Too much and the wood furs, the grain lifts and composite boards get permanently marked. We match the pressure to the material and let the solution handle the growth.

Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. Estimates are free, and the quote is based on the property rather than a price list.

What the job includes

  • Algae, mildew and ground in grime lifted off the boards
  • Pressure matched to wood, composite or vinyl
  • Railings, spindles and steps done with the deck surface
  • A less slippery surface once the growth is gone
  • Ready for sealing or staining if that is the next step

Why Branford is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Branford sits on Long Island Sound roughly five miles east of New Haven, and its shoreline is unusually broken. Off Stony Creek lie the Thimble Islands, an archipelago of pink granite outcrops ranging from bare ledges to islands several acres across. They act as a natural breakwater for the mainland behind them.

Areas of Branford we work in: Stony Creek and the Thimble Islands shore, Indian Neck, Short Beach, Pine Orchard, Branford Point, the town center and the neighborhoods north toward the Branford River.
