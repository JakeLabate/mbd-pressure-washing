How we do it

House washing done properly.

Soft washing puts the work on the cleaning solution rather than on pressure. The solution breaks down the algae and grime, then a low pressure rinse carries it away. That matters on siding because high pressure drives water behind panels, strips paint and chews up trim.

Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. Estimates are free, and the quote is based on the property rather than a price list.

What the job includes

  • Dirt, algae, mildew and weather buildup lifted off the siding
  • Low pressure throughout, so nothing is driven behind the panels
  • Vinyl, stucco, brick, Hardie board and painted clapboard
  • Trim, soffits and gutter exteriors done with the walls
  • Cleaning solution matched to the surface, not one mix for everything

Why Trumbull is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.
