How we do it

Roof washing done properly.

A roof is the one surface where high pressure does the most damage the fastest, because it takes the granules off the shingle along with the dirt. We treat the growth with a professional grade solution at low pressure and let the rinse carry it off.

What the job includes

  • Black streaking, algae, moss and lichen treated and removed
  • Low pressure only, so the granules stay on the shingle
  • Asphalt shingle and similar roofing materials
  • Runoff directed away from planting where practical
  • Routine maintenance or a heavier seasonal clean

Why Trumbull is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Trumbull sits inland in Fairfield County, north of Bridgeport, with the Pequonnock River running down through the middle of it toward Bridgeport Harbor. The Pequonnock River Valley is preserved open space now, rugged ground with granite outcroppings and marsh, and the town keeps more than 1,600 acres in recreation and open space, around 11 percent of its land.
