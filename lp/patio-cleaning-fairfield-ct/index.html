Why Fairfield is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Fairfield has around five miles of shoreline on Long Island Sound and five public beaches along it, from South Pine Creek and Penfield across to Sasco and Southport. Down there the work is salt film and mildew on siding, and a lot of the housing that takes it is old.

Areas of Fairfield we work in: Southport, Sasco Hill, Fairfield Beach, South Benson, Greenfield Hill, Stratfield, the University area and the neighborhoods around downtown.
