How we do it

Fence cleaning done properly.

Most fences that look worn out are not. The grey is algae and weather buildup sitting on top of the material, not the material itself aging. Lifting it off is usually the difference between a fence that looks finished and one that looks neglected.

Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. Estimates are free, and the quote is based on the property rather than a price list.

What the job includes

  • Algae, mildew and weather staining removed panel by panel
  • Vinyl, wood and composite fencing
  • Both sides where access allows
  • Low pressure on vinyl, so the panels are not furred or forced apart
  • Posts, caps and gate hardware done with the run
