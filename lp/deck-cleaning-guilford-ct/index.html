How we do it

Deck cleaning done properly.

Decking is where pressure gets people into trouble. Too much and the wood furs, the grain lifts and composite boards get permanently marked. We match the pressure to the material and let the solution handle the growth.

What the job includes

  • Algae, mildew and ground in grime lifted off the boards
  • Pressure matched to wood, composite or vinyl
  • Railings, spindles and steps done with the deck surface
  • A less slippery surface once the growth is gone
  • Ready for sealing or staining if that is the next step

Why Guilford is like this

Local conditions, not a sales pitch.

Guilford was founded in 1639 and its historic town center covers around four square miles, running from Interstate 95 south to Long Island Sound, with the West River on one side and East Creek on the other. Both edges are still lined with salt marsh, so the center sits back from the Sound across a broad band of marsh rather than directly on sand.

Areas of Guilford we work in: The town green and historic center, Sachem's Head, Leetes Island, Chaffinch Island, Guilford Point and the marina area, plus the neighborhoods north toward the Guilford lakes.
